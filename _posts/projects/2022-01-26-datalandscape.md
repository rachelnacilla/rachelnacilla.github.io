<p><em>Exploring the Modern Data Landscape</em> was a presentation I delivered in May 2021 at the inaugural meetup for Siligong.Data, part of <a href="https://www.siligongvalley.com/" target="_blank">Siligong Valley</a>. This event brought together data professionals from diverse backgrounds, including data scientists, software engineers, business analysts, and marketing professionals. As the opening talk of the session, my presentation provided a concise overview of the current data landscape andhow data is being applied in today's technology industry.</p>
