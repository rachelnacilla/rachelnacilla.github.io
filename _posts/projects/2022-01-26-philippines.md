<p>In 2013, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Haiyan" target="_blank">Typhoon Haiyan</a>, a category 5 typhoon, made landfall in eastern Philippines. It was the deadliest typhoon on record at the time, causing mass destruction in the islands of Leyte and Samar.</p>

<p><em>Providing Hope for the Philippines</em> was a charity event held in Wollongong, NSW in December 2013. As a volunteer member of the organising committee, I created an online infographic using HTML, CSS and JavaScript to encourage the community to donate in support of ongoing relief efforts. In the end, over $6,000 in total was raised that night, with proceeds going towards humanitarian aid.</p> 

<p>At the time, my career focus was in front-end development. While I've since moved into data and analytics engineering, I'm proud of this project and included it in my portfolio as a reflection of both my skills and my commitment to supporting my community, which I remain passionate about to this day.</p>
